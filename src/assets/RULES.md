# Baker’s Dirty Dozen Fantasy Football League

### League Rules and Guidelines	
#### Commissioner: David Chapman	
#### Co-Commissioners: McHenry Pattison, Zak Lepper	
#### Treasurer: Riley Kennemer	

The following are the rules and guidelines for the Baker’s Dirty Dozen Fantasy Football League during the 2024-2025 NFL season. The rules are locked at the beginning of the season and can only be amended mid season via unanimous league vote. Any midseason changes cannot be applied retroactively. The rules will continue on to the 2025 season, and will be up for review after the final regular season game of the 2024 season. By being a member of the league, all members agree to play by these rules.

Each manager is allowed to add a second manager that is not already a part of the league to assist them for the draft if they can’t make it. The second manager may only be present at the draft if the first manager cannot make the draft. The commissioner must be notified prior to the draft of any additional team managers.

### [ 1.2 ] Schedule
The schedule is split into 4 sections. Round Robin, Rivalry Week, Playoff Push and Playoffs. Round Robin takes place over the Rirst 11 weeks where each manager plays each other a single time in a random order. Rivalry Week takes place during week 12. The Playoff Push weeks are weeks 13 and 14. During these weeks, matchups are determined based on who did and didn’t make the playoffs during the prior season. We split the managers into 2 groups of 6 managers. Week 13 everyone is matched up against 1 random person in their group, and week 14 everyone is matched up against 1 random person out of their group. Managers will not face each other twice between weeks 12 and 14.

### [ 1.3 ] Rivalry Week
Week 12	is Rivalry Week. During the offseason, a combination of league history and rivalry survey responses are used to determine rivalry matchups. A full description of this process can be found within the Rivalry Score Calculations document. The first run of matchup selections is determined by the calculations and then modified as needed by commissioners and co-commissioners for optimal selections.

### [ 1.4 ] Matchups, Rosters and Scoring:
Matchups are done on a weekly basis, and the team with the most points wins. Point values are set on Sleeper by the commissioner prior to each season. For a scoring breakdown, see the scoring section in Sleeper. Each team is allowed one quarterback slot, two running back slots, two wide receiver slots, a tight end spot, a flex spot, which can contain a running back, wide receiver, or tight end, a team defense slot, and a kicker slot. Each team gets 7 bench spots as well, making a total roster size of 16 players. The tiebreaker for matchups is most bench points. All matchups will be scored with fractional scoring so that every yard counts. All lineups are final when they lock into place for the week, no exceptions. The commissioner will set no lineup but his own unless accounted for ahead of time.

### [ 1.5 ] Playoffs
Playoffs begin week 15. 6 teams proceed to the playoffs. The top two seeds are automatically advanced to the semi-finals via first round byes. There will be one week per matchup, and the playoffs are single elimination. The tiebreaker for teams entering the playoffs is points for. Each round of the playoffs re-seeds.

### [ 1.6 ] Toilet Bowl
The toilet bowl is the final matchup of the consolation bracket. The consolation bracket works by having teams compete to escape the bracket. The loser of each matchup plays another round until the final matchup during week 17. The loser of the week 17 matchup is the last place team for the year. This carries with it a punishment. The regular season #1 seed gets to dictate the last place team’s team image and team name for the coming season. This punishment is over once either the last place team beats the #1 seed or a new last place team is determined. If a team goes from last to first in consecutive years it is technically possible for a team to win the trophy under the dictated team name. In this instance, the dictated team name will be used for trophy engraving and records. The commissioner and co-commissioners have veto power over team names and images selected by the #1 seed.


## 2. Draft

### [ 2.1 ] Draft Order
The draft order is established by a method chosen by the previous season’s champion. No method can be repeated. The draft order can be vetoed by 2/3 commissioner’s vote.

### [ 2.2 ] Draft Details
The draft will be an online draft. If a manager cannot make the draft then they should attempt to designate someone that is not in the league to pick their team for them. If they do not select any of these methods then they will be auto drafted. It is important to be present for the draft. The draft is 16 rounds, in a snake order. Team managers get 120 seconds per pick, and will be auto drafted if they fail to make a pick before time runs out.

### [ 2.4 ] Missing the Draft
If an owner can’t make the draft, they must designate someone not in the league to draft. If an auto-drafted team wins, payout is adjusted so that: 

- $110 goes to the winner.
- $110 goes to the second place team.

In the event that both owners were auto-drafted, each of the remaining 	10 owners in the league are refunded $10, and the remaining $120 is split equally between the 1st and 2nd place Rinishers.


## 3. Trades and Acquisitions	

### [ 3.1 ] Trades
There is no limit to the amount of trades that teams can be involved in during the season. All 3+ way trades should be submitted to the commissioner to be carried out, but will not be put into action until all involved team managers contact the commissioner themselves. Trades are up for review for a 24-hour period in which 5 votes (half of the league not involved in the trade) will veto the trade. Note that trades should only be vetoed if foul play is obvious.

### [ 3.2 ] Trades – Deadlines
The trade deadline will be the completion of week 13.

### [ 3.3 ] Acquisitions
There is no limit to the number of free agent acquisitions that can be made throughout the season. Waiver order will be the opposite of that weeks season standings. If scoring changes affect the waiver order after waiver claims have been submitted, no new waiver order will be enacted; Waiver claims will stand.


## 4. Other	

### [ 4.1 ] Payment
League fees are $30 per team. The Rirst $60 goes to the trophy engraving and trophy maintenance fund. $10 go to the top scoring team of weeks 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, and 17. The remaining prize money will be paid to the winning manager. Payment must be made to the league treasurer prior to the draft. If there is some reason a manager can’t pay on time, they should contact the commissioner or treasurer as soon as possible.

### [ 4.2 ] Trophy
At the end of the season, the league champion will receive the league’s trophy once it has been engraved with the champion’s personal and team names. The trophy remains with the league champion until the following season’s winner is decided.

### [ 4.3 ] Invalid Lineups
An invalid lineup is defined as a lineup that contains a player who was announced as not playing at least 1 calendar day prior to their game. This protects against late scratches, or morning injury report players. If an owner has 2 invalid lineups in a single season, that owner is automatically nominated for league removal. 6 out of 11 votes removes the owner.
